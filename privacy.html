Privacy Notice

This notice explains how the IImperialism! website handles personal data when you visit the site, use links on the site, and choose whether to allow analytics cookies.

Scope

This notice applies to the public website for IImperialism!, including the landing page, download links, documentation links, and the privacy controls presented on this website.

The site is intended to provide information about the game and let visitors launch the browser-based emulator, download the disk image, download the manual, and visit the public source repository.

Data We Process

Category Source Purpose
Server and delivery logs Your browser, network, and hosting infrastructure Deliver the website, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical issues.
Analytics events Your browser, only after you accept analytics Measure visits and key actions such as emulator launches, downloads, and repository clicks.
Consent preference Your browser local storage Remember whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies.

Cookies And Similar Technologies

The website uses a consent choice stored in your browser under the key iimperialism-analytics-consent. This preference is used to remember whether analytics may run on your device.

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics may set measurement cookies or similar identifiers. If you decline, analytics storage remains denied and Google Analytics should not use analytics cookies for measurement.

You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy settings button in the website footer.

Legal Bases

  • Website delivery, security, and basic operation are processed to provide the site you requested.
  • Analytics cookies and analytics measurement run only on the basis of your consent.
  • You may withdraw analytics consent at any time through the site's privacy controls.

Google Analytics

If you accept analytics, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand general traffic patterns and measure important actions on the site. The tracked actions currently include launching the browser emulator, downloading the disk image, downloading the manual, and opening the source repository.

The analytics implementation uses denied-by-default consent settings. The Google tag loads on page view so consent status can be communicated correctly, but analytics storage remains denied unless you actively opt in.

Google acts as a separate recipient of analytics data. Google's own privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Recipients And International Transfers

Personal data may be processed by the website host, CDN, and related infrastructure providers that are needed to deliver the site securely. If you accept analytics, data is also shared with Google Analytics.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. Where international transfers occur, they are intended to rely on the transfer mechanisms offered by the relevant provider, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual safeguards where required.

Retention

  • Consent choice data remains in your browser until you clear site data or change the setting.
  • Operational logs are retained only for as long as reasonably needed for security, delivery, and troubleshooting.
  • Analytics data is intended to be retained for no longer than 14 months in Google Analytics, unless a shorter period is configured.

Your Rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent for analytics processing.

To exercise privacy rights for this website, contact taciano.perez@gmail.com. You may also complain to the data protection authority in the place where you live, work, or believe a data protection issue occurred.

Changes To This Notice

This notice may be updated when the website, analytics setup, or legal requirements change. The version on this page is the current version for the website.
